Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Horizon Europe grants, success fees, and timelines.

How does EuroHorizon Advisory charge for its services?

We operate on a hybrid model consisting of a modest upfront onboarding & writing fee to cover expert researcher hours, combined with a success-based bonus upon grant award.

What is the average duration of a Horizon Europe proposal preparation?

Typically, a robust EIC Accelerator or Pillar II proposal requires 6 to 10 weeks of intensive drafting, financial planning, and partner alignment before the EC deadline.

Can startups from non-EU countries apply?

Yes! Countries associated with Horizon Europe (such as Ukraine, Moldova, Norway, UK, etc.) enjoy full participation rights on identical terms as EU member states.

What happens if our proposal is rejected on the first attempt?

We analyze the Evaluation Summary Report (ESR), address evaluator criticisms, and resubmit in the subsequent cut-off with prioritized support.
